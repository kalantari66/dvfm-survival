\subsection{Hyperparameter tuning}
\label{app:hyperparameter_settings}

We tuned hyperparameters separately for each source dataset on a
development-only semi-synthetic cohort. The cohort was generated with seed 99,
a Gaussian copula, Kendall's $\tau=0.50$, and the source dataset's observed
censoring rate. It was split once into 70\% training and 30\% validation data,
stratified jointly by event status and observed-time bin. No test partition was
used during tuning, and the development cohort was not reused in the final
ten-seed evaluation.

CoxPH was retained at its unpenalized implementation defaults. For every other
learner, we independently sampled $R=10$ configurations from the
model-specific discrete candidate set $\mathcal{C}_m$ in Table
\ref{tab:semi_synthetic_search_spaces}. Each candidate was fitted on the
development training partition and scored by its oracle integrated Brier score
(IBS) on the validation partition. The selected configuration was
\[
  c_m^* = \arg\min_{c \in \{c_1,\ldots,c_R\} \subset \mathcal{C}_m}
  \operatorname{IBS}_{\mathrm{oracle}}^{\mathrm{val}}(c; m).
\]
Oracle IBS is available because the semi-synthetic DGP supplies the true
event-time survival distribution; it was used instead of an IPCW criterion,
which would require an independent-censoring assumption. Failed configurations
were treated as infeasible and excluded from selection. The selected settings
were then fixed for all final scenarios and repeats. DVFM was tuned with a
one-dimensional latent variable ($z=1$), and the resulting architecture and
optimization settings were shared with its $z=0$ ablation.

All candidates used the implementation's remaining fixed settings. In
particular, DeepSurv, MTLR, and Clayton--Weibull AFT used their configured
fixed epoch budgets; HACSurv-2D and Bayesian Cox--Gamma frailty used
validation-based early stopping; and DVFM trained for its fixed epoch budget
while retaining the best eligible post-warm-up validation-ELBO checkpoint.
